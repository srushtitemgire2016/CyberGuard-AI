CYBERSECURITY INCIDENT RESPONSE

Incident response is the organized process for handling suspected or confirmed security events.

Common phases:
1. Preparation: define roles, contacts, backups, logging, and response procedures.
2. Detection and analysis: assess alerts, determine scope, preserve evidence, and distinguish incidents from false positives.
3. Containment: limit spread and ongoing harm.
4. Eradication: remove the cause, such as malware or compromised access.
5. Recovery: restore services safely and monitor for recurrence.
6. Lessons learned: document what happened and improve controls.

General practices:
- Escalate promptly according to organizational policy.
- Record times, affected systems, actions, and decisions.
- Preserve relevant logs and evidence.
- Avoid destroying evidence or making unsupported assumptions.
- Communicate through approved channels.
- Meet applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements.
- Test the response plan and update it after exercises or incidents.

The correct technical steps depend on the incident. Critical environments should involve qualified incident responders.

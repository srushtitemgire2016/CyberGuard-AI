BACKUP AND DISASTER RECOVERY

Backups help restore data after ransomware, accidental deletion, hardware failure, or other incidents.

Good practices:
- Define recovery time objectives and recovery point objectives.
- Keep multiple copies, including offline or immutable backups where feasible.
- Separate backup credentials and access from ordinary production accounts.
- Encrypt backups and protect keys.
- Monitor backup jobs and alert on failures.
- Test restoration regularly, not merely backup creation.
- Document recovery order and responsibilities.
- Protect backups from deletion by compromised administrator accounts.
- Keep disaster-recovery procedures current.

A backup that has never been restored in a test may not be usable. Recovery planning should include applications, configurations, identity services, and data dependencies.

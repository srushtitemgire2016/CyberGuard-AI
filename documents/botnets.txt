BOTNETS

A botnet is a group of devices compromised and controlled by an operator. Infected devices may be used for spam, credential attacks, DDoS, or other abuse.

Devices may be infected through weak passwords, unpatched software, malicious downloads, or exposed services.

Possible signs:
- Unusual outbound traffic or repeated connections to unknown hosts.
- Unexpected resource use.
- Unknown processes or configuration changes.
- Security alerts from network or endpoint tools.

Prevention and response:
- Change default device credentials and use strong unique passwords.
- Patch routers, cameras, servers, and other connected devices.
- Disable remote administration when unnecessary.
- Segment IoT devices from sensitive systems.
- Use trusted security tools and monitor network traffic.
- Isolate suspected devices and follow vendor or security-team remediation guidance.
- Factory-reset or reinstall only when appropriate, then patch and securely reconfigure.

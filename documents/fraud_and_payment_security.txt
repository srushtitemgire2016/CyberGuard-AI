ONLINE FRAUD AND PAYMENT SECURITY

Online fraud can involve impersonation, fake invoices, payment redirection, account takeover, investment scams, and fraudulent support messages.

Warning signs:
- Urgent requests to change bank details or make an unusual payment.
- Requests for OTPs, PINs, passwords, or remote access.
- Unverified payment links or QR codes.
- Unexpected refunds, prizes, or investment guarantees.
- Pressure to keep a transaction secret.

Protection:
- Verify payment changes using a known phone number or contact method.
- Require a second approver for significant transfers.
- Review recipient details before confirming payment.
- Never share banking credentials or OTPs.
- Use transaction alerts and strong account authentication.
- Contact the bank immediately if a suspicious transaction occurs.
- Preserve messages, receipts, and transaction identifiers for reporting.

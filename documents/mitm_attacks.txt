MAN-IN-THE-MIDDLE (MITM) ATTACKS

A man-in-the-middle attack occurs when an attacker intercepts or alters communication between parties who believe they are communicating directly.

Related risks include rogue Wi-Fi access points, DNS manipulation, compromised routers, and downgrade or certificate-validation failures.

Protection:
- Use HTTPS and never ignore browser certificate warnings.
- Keep devices, browsers, and router firmware updated.
- Avoid sensitive activity on untrusted networks unless using properly configured secure connections.
- Use a trusted VPN when appropriate, understanding that a VPN shifts trust to its provider and does not replace HTTPS.
- Use MFA and monitor account activity.
- Verify unexpected changes to payment or account details through another channel.
- Secure home and office Wi-Fi with WPA2-AES or WPA3, strong router administrator credentials, and updated firmware.

If interception is suspected, stop entering sensitive information, switch to a trusted network, and contact the affected service or security team.

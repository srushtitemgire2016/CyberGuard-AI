DATA BREACHES AND DATA EXPOSURE

A data breach is an incident in which information is accessed, disclosed, altered, lost, or stolen without authorization. Exposure can occur through attacks, mistakes, misconfiguration, or lost devices.

Sensitive data may include personal information, credentials, financial records, health information, source code, or business secrets.

Prevention:
- Collect and retain only data that is needed.
- Classify data and restrict access.
- Encrypt data at rest and in transit.
- Use secure backups and access logging.
- Review cloud sharing and database exposure.
- Remove secrets from source code and rotate exposed credentials.
- Securely dispose of data and devices.

Response:
- Contain the exposure and preserve evidence.
- Determine what data and systems are affected.
- Revoke or rotate exposed credentials and keys.
- Engage legal, privacy, security, and leadership teams as appropriate.
- Follow applicable notification laws and contractual requirements.
- Inform affected people through verified channels when required.
- Correct the root cause and monitor for misuse.

IDENTITY AND ACCESS MANAGEMENT

Identity and access management (IAM) ensures that the right identities have appropriate access to the right resources.

Principles:
- Least privilege: grant only the permissions necessary for a task.
- Need to know: limit access to information required for a role.
- Separation of duties: avoid concentrating incompatible powers in one identity.
- Zero trust: continuously verify access using identity, device, context, and policy rather than assuming a network location is safe.
- Joiner-mover-leaver processes: grant, adjust, and remove access as people change roles or leave.

Good practices:
- Use role-based access control where suitable.
- Require MFA for privileged access.
- Review access regularly.
- Use separate administrative accounts.
- Protect service accounts and API credentials.
- Remove stale accounts and tokens.
- Log privileged actions and investigate unusual access.

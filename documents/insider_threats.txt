INSIDER THREATS

An insider threat involves risk from someone with authorized access, including employees, contractors, vendors, or other trusted users. It may be malicious, negligent, or accidental.

Examples:
- Intentional theft or disclosure of confidential information.
- Accidental sending of data to the wrong recipient.
- Misuse of privileges.
- Compromised accounts used by an external attacker.
- Unsafe handling of removable media or cloud-sharing permissions.

Controls:
- Apply least privilege and role-based access.
- Review access periodically and remove access promptly when roles change.
- Separate duties for sensitive actions.
- Log and monitor access to critical data proportionately and lawfully.
- Use data-loss prevention where appropriate.
- Train users on data classification and safe sharing.
- Provide clear reporting channels and investigate fairly.
- Avoid treating a single unusual behavior as proof of malicious intent.

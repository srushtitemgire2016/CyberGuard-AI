IDENTITY THEFT AND ACCOUNT TAKEOVER

Identity theft involves unauthorized use of another person's identifying information. Account takeover occurs when someone gains control of an account without permission.

Common routes:
- Phishing and social engineering.
- Reused or stolen passwords.
- Malware that steals information.
- Data breaches and exposed personal information.
- SIM-swap or fraudulent account-recovery attacks.

Prevention:
- Use unique passwords or passkeys and enable MFA.
- Limit the personal information shared publicly.
- Secure email accounts because they often control password resets.
- Set account alerts and review transactions and sign-in history.
- Protect mobile carrier accounts with available PINs or safeguards.
- Verify requests for identity documents and financial information.

If affected:
- Secure the email account and affected accounts from a trusted device.
- Change exposed credentials and revoke unknown sessions.
- Contact banks, providers, and relevant authorities promptly.
- Preserve messages and transaction records.
- Follow official identity-theft reporting and recovery procedures for your country.

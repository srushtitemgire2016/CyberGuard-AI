ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND CYBERSECURITY

AI can support defensive work such as summarizing logs, classifying alerts, assisting security awareness, and organizing incident reports. AI outputs may be incorrect and should be verified.

AI-related risks include:
- Deepfake audio or video used for impersonation and fraud.
- AI-generated phishing messages.
- Prompt injection, where untrusted content attempts to manipulate an AI system.
- Sensitive data exposure through prompts, logs, or connected tools.
- Insecure AI integrations and excessive tool permissions.
- Overreliance on generated security advice.

Defenses:
- Verify high-impact requests through independent channels.
- Do not submit secrets or sensitive personal data to unapproved services.
- Treat retrieved documents and external content as untrusted input.
- Keep AI tools within least-privilege boundaries.
- Require human review for consequential actions.
- Log and test AI system behavior while protecting sensitive data.
- Validate generated commands, code, and recommendations before use.
- Separate model-generated suggestions from confirmed security findings.
